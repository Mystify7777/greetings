07 · THE PART I DON'T WANT TO PRETEND DIDN'T HAPPEN

Ek baat kehni thi.

Hum dono se galti hui.

Jo kuch humare beech hua, usmein sirf tum galat nahi thi. Main bhi tha. Kaafi baar.

Main apni hurt feelings aur apni baat ko lekar itna rigid ho gaya ki jab tumne ek baar baat karne ki koshish ki, maine tumhe hi door kar diya.

Shayad us waqt mujhe thoda ruk jaana chahiye tha. Tumhe samajhne ki koshish karni chahiye thi, sirf apna point prove nahi karna chahiye tha.

Uske liye sorry. Sach mein.

Main yeh page tumse kuch maangne ke liye nahi likh raha. Na forgiveness. Na reply. Na kuch fix karne ka pressure.

Bas kuch cheezein thi jo kehni reh gayi thi.
