07 · THE PART I DON'T WANT TO PRETEND DIDN'T HAPPEN

Ek baat kehni thi.

Hum dono se galti hui.

Jo kuch humare beech hua, usmein sirf tum galat nahi thi. Main bhi tha. Kaafi baar.

Main apni hurt feelings aur apni baat ko lekar itna rigid ho gaya ki jab tumne ek baar baat karne ki koshish ki, maine tumhe hi door kar diya.

Shayad us waqt mujhe thoda ruk jaana chahiye tha. Tumhe samajhne ki koshish karni chahiye thi, sirf apna point prove nahi karna chahiye tha.

Uske liye sorry. Sach mein.

Main yeh page tumse kuch maangne ke liye nahi likh raha. Na forgiveness. Na reply. Na kuch fix karne ka pressure.

Bas kuch cheezein thi jo kehni reh gayi thi.

09 · THE NEXT PAGE

Shayad kahani yahin khatam nahi hoti.

Abhi sab kuch
pehle jaisa nahi hai.

Ab hum phir se baat kar rahe hain. Bas beech mein jo distance aa gaya tha, woh abhi bhi kahin na kahin hai.

Kabhi-kabhi lagta hai ki ek dusre ko jaante hue bhi thode strangers jaise ho gaye hain. Aur shayad theek hai.

Har cheez ko turant pehle jaisa banana zaroori nahi hota. Kabhi-kabhi bas baat dobara shuru ho jaana hi kaafi hota hai.

Isliye is chapter mein koi promise nahi hai. Koi expectation bhi nahi.

Bas ek chhoti si hope hai ki aage kabhi phir kuch naye moments ban sakein. Naye jokes. Nayi bakwaas. Kuch random plans. Kuch aur photos jo bina kisi reason ke save ho jaayein.

Aur shayad, dheere-dheere, woh comfort bhi wapas aaye jo kabhi itna normal lagta tha.

Abhi kahani exactly kya hai, pata nahi.

Bas page blank nahi hai.
Jo aage likha jayega, woh purani kahani ko repeat karna zaroori nahi hai. It can be something new. ♡
